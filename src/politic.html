The type of information we collect depends on how an individual interacts with Tesmeria.

• Email address;
• Telephone number;
• Job title or professional role;

5. How We Use Personal Information

Tesmeria may process personal information for the following purposes:
• To respond to inquiries, messages and requests;
• To arrange consultations and discuss potential projects;
• To provide information about our marketing and consulting services;
• To prepare proposals, quotations or scopes of work;
• To establish, manage and perform client relationships;
• To deliver agreed services and communicate about active projects;
• To maintain business, administrative and accounting records;
• To improve the website, services and user experience;
• To protect the website and detect technical, fraudulent or security-related activity;
• To comply with legal, regulatory, accounting or contractual obligations;
• To establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
• To send marketing communications where the recipient has provided consent or where otherwise permitted by applicable law.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the reason for which it was originally collected unless there is an appropriate legal basis to do so.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, personal information may be processed on one or more of the following grounds:
• The consent of the data subject;
• The need to take steps at the individual’s request before entering into a contract;
• The conclusion or performance of a contract;
• Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations;
• The protection of the Company’s legitimate business interests, provided that such interests do not override the individual’s fundamental rights;
• The establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

Where processing is based on consent, the individual may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting info@tesmeria.com.
Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the consent was withdrawn.

Tesmeria does not sell or rent personal information.

• Recipients may include:

Personal information may be processed or stored outside Costa Rica when Tesmeria uses international hosting, cloud storage, email, communication or other technology providers.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the information receives an appropriate level of protection and is handled in accordance with applicable Costa Rican data protection requirements.

The level of data protection in another country may differ from that available in Costa Rica. Where appropriate, Tesmeria may use contractual, organizational or technical safeguards to protect transferred information.

9. Data Retention

Tesmeria retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The applicable retention period may depend on:
• The nature of the information;
• The purpose for which it was collected;
• The duration of the business or client relationship;
• Contractual requirements;
• Legal, accounting or regulatory obligations;
• The need to resolve disputes or defend legal claims.

When personal information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymized or securely archived where continued retention is legally permitted or required.

10. Data Security

Tesmeria applies reasonable administrative, organizational and technical measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, electronic communication or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Individuals should avoid sending highly sensitive information through standard email or general website forms.

• Request that unauthorized or unlawful processing be stopped;

Requests may be submitted to info@tesmeria.com.
