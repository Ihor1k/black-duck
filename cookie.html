Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how BLACK DUCK ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on blackduckpay.com (the "Website"). This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember certain preferences, and provide information about how visitors use the website. Similar technologies, including browser local storage, may also be used for related purposes.

03

How We Use Cookies

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • to support essential Website functionality;
  • to remember certain user preferences;
  • to understand how visitors interact with the Website;
  • to measure Website traffic and performance;
  • to help us improve the Website and user experience.

We do not use cookies to sell personal information.

04

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Website and to help us measure and improve its performance. Google Analytics may collect information such as: pages visited; session activity; approximate location; browser and device information; referral information; interaction with Website content. The information collected through Google Analytics is processed in accordance with Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices.

05

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies
These cookies or similar technologies are required for basic Website functionality and cannot normally be disabled through our Website.

Analytics Cookies
Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Website, including which pages are visited and how users navigate through the Website. We use Google Analytics for this purpose. Where required by applicable law, analytics cookies are activated only after the user has provided the appropriate consent.

06

Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, we request your consent before using non-essential cookies, including analytics cookies. You may accept or reject non-essential cookies through the cookie notice or consent controls provided on the Website. Your choice may be stored in your browser so that the Website can remember your preference.

07

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. You may also change your cookie preferences through the Website’s consent controls where available. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some Website features operate.

08

Third-Party Services

Some Website functionality may be provided by third-party services that use their own cookies or similar technologies. Google Analytics is currently one such service. If additional third-party services are introduced in the future, this Cookie Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

09

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Website, the technologies we use, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page together with the revised "Last updated" date.
