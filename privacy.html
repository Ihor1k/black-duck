Introduction

BLACK DUCK ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit blackduckpay.com (the "Website"), how we use that information, when it may be shared, and the rights that may be available to you under applicable data protection laws.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including your name, company name, email address, phone number, website or business information, and the content of enquiries or other communications you send to us.

We also collect certain technical and usage information when you visit the Website. This may include browser and device information, session activity, pages visited, referral information, approximate location, and information about how you interact with the Website.

Some of this information is collected through Google Analytics and similar technologies used to understand Website usage and performance.

03

How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and communicate with you;
  • understand your business or payment requirements;
  • provide information about our services;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Website;
  • prevent misuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

04

Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires us to identify a legal basis for processing, we may rely on:

  • your consent;
  • our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Website and responding to business enquiries;
  • steps taken at your request before entering into a business relationship;
  • compliance with legal or regulatory obligations.

The legal basis used will depend on the circumstances in which the information is collected and processed.

05

Sharing of Personal Information

We may share personal information with service providers that support our business and Website operations, including hosting, communications, technical support, security, and professional service providers.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us and are expected to handle it appropriately.

We may also disclose personal information where required by law, regulation, legal process, or competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, systems, users, or business.

06

International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you are located.

Where required by applicable law, we take appropriate steps to ensure that international transfers of personal information are subject to suitable safeguards.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records, meeting legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymised, or otherwise securely disposed of.

08

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection or supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.
