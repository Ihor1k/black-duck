Connected payment flow

One transaction. Several connected systems.

A customer sees a payment as one simple action, but several systems can be involved behind it. The payment request moves from your website, application, or payment link through the gateway and processing infrastructure before reaching the relevant bank or card network and returning a transaction response.

Gateway connectivity

The connection behind online payments.

The payment gateway connects the customer-facing payment experience with the providers responsible for handling the transaction. It passes payment requests and responses between systems and helps bring checkout and processing into one connected flow.
