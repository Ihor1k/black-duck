Payments should fit the way business happens.

A payment is one moment in a much larger operation. Before it reaches a customer, a business has to consider where the payment starts, which providers are involved, which currencies it needs to handle, and where the funds go next. BLACK DUCK approaches payment services through that wider picture.

The bigger picture

Start with the transaction.
Look beyond it.

Two companies may both accept online payments and still need very different setups. One may be focused on a smooth checkout. Another may need to pay international suppliers, manage several currencies, or connect payments to an existing platform. The right questions depend on the business behind the transaction.

Layer 01

The customer journey

Where and how does a customer pay? A website checkout, an application, and a payment link each create a different experience and different integration needs.

Layer 02

The movement of funds

Accepting a payment is only part of the picture. Businesses also need to consider processing, payouts, currencies, and the destinations involved.

Layer 03

The existing environment

Payment services have to work within a company’s technical and operational setup. The connections between systems matter as much as the individual components.

Connected payments

Different needs. One connected conversation.

Payment requirements often overlap. A business expanding into a new market may need to think about customer payments and local currencies at the same time. A digital platform may need a checkout experience that works with its product and a way to move funds to business recipients. Looking at each requirement together helps reveal the connections that matter.

BLACK DUCK brings online payments, card payments, processing, payouts, multi-currency activity, payment links, and gateway connectivity into that conversation. The focus is on understanding how these elements could work together for a specific business need.
